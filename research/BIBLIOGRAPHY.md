# Bibliography — living

*Started 2026-11-15. Every paper dossier in `papers/` adds its full citation here.
Claims in dossiers are only as good as these sources; links are to the canonical
record (arXiv / venue / publisher).*

## Runner & serving systems

- Kwon, W., Li, Z., Zhuang, S., Sheng, Y., Zheng, L., Yu, C. H., Gonzalez, J. E., Zhang, H., & Stoica, I. (2023). *Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention.* SOSP '23, 611–626. arXiv:2309.06180. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180 — KV cache managed as OS-style paged memory (fixed blocks + block tables + copy-on-write sharing) removes fragmentation waste and lifts serving throughput 2–4×. Dossier: `papers/pagedattention-vllm.md`.
- Yu, G.-I., Jeong, J. S., Kim, G.-W., Kim, S., & Chun, B.-G. (2022). *Orca: A Distributed Serving System for Transformer-Based Generative Models.* OSDI '22, 521–538. https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi22/presentation/yu — iteration-level scheduling (continuous batching) plus selective batching. (No arXiv version.) Dossier: `papers/orca.md`.
- Zheng, L., Yin, L., Xie, Z., Sun, C., Huang, J., Yu, C. H., Cao, S., Kozyrakis, C., Stoica, I., Gonzalez, J. E., Barrett, C., & Sheng, Y. (2024). *SGLang: Efficient Execution of Structured Language Model Programs.* NeurIPS 37. arXiv:2312.07104. https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07104 — RadixAttention keeps finished requests' KV in an LRU radix tree keyed by tokens, so shared prefixes are reused automatically, with cache-aware scheduling on top. Dossier: `papers/radixattention-sglang.md`.

## KV cache, attention, decoding

- Gim, I., Chen, G., Lee, S.-s., Sarda, N., Khandelwal, A., & Zhong, L. (2024). *Prompt Cache: Modular Attention Reuse for Low-Latency Inference.* MLSys 2024. arXiv:2311.04934. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04934 — reusable prompt segments declared as modules with precomputed attention states, spliced into new prompts to cut TTFT; largest gains on CPU. Dossier: `papers/prompt-cache.md`.
- Yao, J., Li, H., Liu, Y., Ray, S., Cheng, Y., Zhang, Q., Du, K., Lu, S., & Jiang, J. (2025). *CacheBlend: Fast Large Language Model Serving for RAG with Cached Knowledge Fusion.* EuroSys '25. DOI: 10.1145/3689031.3696098. arXiv:2405.16444. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16444 — reuses precomputed KV for chunks in any position and repairs cross-attention by recomputing only the highest-deviation ~15% of tokens. Dossier: `papers/cacheblend.md`.
- Xiao, G., Tian, Y., Chen, B., Han, S., & Lewis, M. (2024). *Efficient Streaming Language Models with Attention Sinks.* ICLR 2024. arXiv:2309.17453. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17453 — permanently pinning ~4 initial tokens' KV (attention sinks) plus a sliding window gives constant-memory, stable streaming. Directly supports our character-anchor law. Dossier: `papers/streamingllm.md`.
- Zhang, Z., Sheng, Y., Zhou, T., Chen, T., Zheng, L., Cai, R., Song, Z., Tian, Y., Ré, C., Barrett, C., Wang, Z., & Chen, B. (2023). *H2O: Heavy-Hitter Oracle for Efficient Generative Inference of Large Language Models.* NeurIPS 36, 34661–34710. arXiv:2306.14048. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14048 — a fixed KV budget split between heavy-hitter tokens (by accumulated attention) and recent tokens preserves quality at ~20% of the full cache. Dossier: `papers/h2o.md`.

### KV compression & attention-architecture memory reduction (wave 3)

- Liu, Z., Yuan, J., Jin, H., Zhong, S., Xu, Z., Braverman, V., Chen, B., & Hu, X. (2024). *KIVI: A Tuning-Free Asymmetric 2bit Quantization for KV Cache.* ICML 2024, PMLR 235, 32332–32344. arXiv:2402.02750. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02750 — keys quantized per-channel, values per-token; 2-bit KV with a 128-token FP16 residual window matches FP16 quality at 2.6× less peak memory. Dossier: `papers/kivi.md`.
- Hooper, C., Kim, S., Mohammadzadeh, H., Mahoney, M. W., Shao, Y. S., Keutzer, K., & Gholami, A. (2024). *KVQuant: Towards 10 Million Context Length LLM Inference with KV Cache Quantization.* NeurIPS 37. arXiv:2401.18079. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18079 — pre-RoPE per-channel key quantization + Fisher-weighted non-uniform datatypes hold 3-bit KV to <0.1 PPL degradation. Dossier: `papers/kvquant.md`.
- Li, Y., Huang, Y., Yang, B., Venkitesh, B., Locatelli, A., Ye, H., Cai, T., Lewis, P., & Chen, D. (2024). *SnapKV: LLM Knows What You are Looking for Before Generation.* NeurIPS 37, 22947–22970. arXiv:2404.14469. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14469 — per-head prompt-KV selection voted from the final observation window; constant-size prompt cache, 8.2× memory efficiency at 16K on A100. Dossier: `papers/snapkv.md`.
- Cai, Z., Zhang, Y., Gao, B., Liu, Y., Li, Y., Liu, T., Lu, K., Xiong, W., Dong, Y., Hu, J., & Xiao, W. (2025). *PyramidKV: Dynamic KV Cache Compression based on Pyramidal Information Funneling.* COLM 2025. arXiv:2406.02069. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02069 — layer-decreasing (pyramidal) cache budgets match full-cache LongBench at 12% retention. Dossier: `papers/pyramidkv.md`.
- Ainslie, J., Lee-Thorp, J., de Jong, M., Zemlyanskiy, Y., Lebrón, F., & Sanghai, S. (2023). *GQA: Training Generalized Multi-Query Transformer Models from Multi-Head Checkpoints.* EMNLP 2023, 4895–4901. arXiv:2305.13245. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13245 — G query groups share KV heads, cutting KV by H/G vs MHA; uptrained conversion at ~5% of original compute. A model property, not a runtime policy. Dossier: `papers/gqa.md`.
- DeepSeek-AI. (2024). *DeepSeek-V2: A Strong, Economical, and Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Language Model.* arXiv:2405.04434. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04434 — Multi-head Latent Attention caches a 512-dim latent + 64-dim RoPE key per token per layer, cutting KV 93.3% vs DeepSeek 67B while beating MHA in ablations. Technical report (no conference venue). Dossier: `papers/deepseek-v2-mla.md`.

### Attention kernels (wave 4)

- Dao, T., Fu, D. Y., Ermon, S., Rudra, A., & Ré, C. (2022). *FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness.* NeurIPS 35. arXiv:2205.14135. https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135 — IO-aware exact attention: SRAM tiling + online softmax never materialise the N×N matrix in HBM; backward recomputation trades ~13% more FLOPs for ~9× less traffic. Dossier: `papers/flashattention.md`.
- Dao, T. (2024). *FlashAttention-2: Faster Attention with Better Parallelism and Work Partitioning.* ICLR 2024. arXiv:2307.08691. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691 — deferred rescaling, sequence-parallel loop order, split-Q warp partitioning; 50–73% of A100 peak. (arXiv July 2023; ICLR 2024 is the venue of record.) Dossier: `papers/flashattention-2.md`.
- Shah, J., Bikshandi, G., Zhang, Y., Thakkar, V., Ramani, P., & Dao, T. (2024). *FlashAttention-3: Fast and Accurate Attention with Asynchrony and Low-precision.* NeurIPS 37. arXiv:2407.08608. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08608 — Hopper-only: warp-specialised async + FP8 with block quantisation and incoherent processing; ~1.2 PFLOPs/s FP8 on H100. No runnable kernel for Turing — precision lessons only. Dossier: `papers/flashattention-3.md`.
- Ye, Z., Chen, L., Lai, R., Lin, W., Zhang, Y., Wang, S., Chen, T., Kasikci, B., Grover, V., Krishnamurthy, A., & Ceze, L. (2025). *FlashInfer: Efficient and Customizable Attention Engine for LLM Inference Serving.* MLSys 2025. arXiv:2501.01005. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01005 — the lineage as serving infrastructure: unified block-sparse KV layouts, JIT kernel templates from Turing (sm75) to Hopper, plan/run scheduler; inside SGLang, vLLM, MLC-Engine. Dossier: `papers/flashinfer.md`.

### Long context / RoPE scaling (wave 7)

- Peng, B., Quesnelle, J., Fan, H., & Shippole, E. (2024). *YaRN: Efficient Context Window Extension of Large Language Models.* ICLR 2024. arXiv:2309.00071. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00071 — NTK-by-parts interpolation plus attention temperature extends Llama 2 from 4k to 128k with ~10× fewer fine-tuning tokens than prior methods; shipped as the `rope_scaling: yarn` option in llama.cpp/vLLM/HF and Qwen-class model cards. Dossier: `papers/yarn.md`.
- Ding, Y., Zhang, L. L., Zhang, C., Xu, Y., Shang, N., Xu, J., Yang, F., & Yang, M. (2024). *LongRoPE: Extending LLM Context Window Beyond 2 Million Tokens.* ICML 2024, PMLR 235:11091–11104. arXiv:2402.13753. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13753 — evolutionary search over per-dimension RoPE rescaling; shipped in production at 128k inside Phi-3, not at its 2048k headline (perplexity + single-passkey only above 256k — and the lab's own LongRoPE2 follow-up found large effective-length shortfalls). Dossier: `papers/longrope.md`.

### Mixture-of-experts (wave 6)

- Jiang, A. Q., et al. (2024). *Mixtral of Experts.* arXiv technical report. arXiv:2401.04088. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04088 — 8 experts, top-2 routing: 46.7B total / 12.9B active per token; memory cost follows the total count while compute follows the active count; the paper specifies no load-balancing loss and no serving measurements. Dossier: `papers/mixtral.md`.
- Xue, L., Fu, Y., Lu, Z., Sun, C., Mai, L., & Marina, M. (2025). *MoE-Infinity: Efficient MoE Inference on Personal Machines with Sparsity-Aware Expert Cache.* arXiv:2401.14361 (v3, 12 March 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14361 — expert reuse is skewed within a request but uniform across requests, so the GPU expert cache is driven by request-level activation traces; 3.1–16.7× per-token latency improvements on a single A5000-24GB (v3 abstract figures; its conclusion reports 2.7–13.7× — both recorded in the dossier). Dossier: `papers/moe-infinity.md`.

### Weight quantization (wave 5)

- Frantar, E., Ashkboos, S., Hoefler, T., & Alistarh, D. (2023). *GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers.* ICLR 2023. arXiv:2210.17323. https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323 — one-shot layer-wise quantization with Hessian error compensation (Cholesky-reformulated); 175B to 3–4 bits in ~4 GPU-hours, near-FP16 perplexity at 4 bits. Dossier: `papers/gptq.md`.
- Lin, J., Tang, J., Tang, H., Yang, S., Chen, W.-M., Wang, W.-C., Xiao, G., Dang, X., Gan, C., & Han, S. (2024). *AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization for LLM Compression and Acceleration.* MLSys 2024. arXiv:2306.00978. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00978 — the ~1% salient channels are found by activation magnitude and protected by searched per-channel scaling inside a uniform grouped format; far less calibration overfitting than GPTQ. Dossier: `papers/awq.md`.
- Xiao, G., Lin, J., Seznec, M., Wu, H., Demouth, J., & Han, S. (2023). *SmoothQuant: Accurate and Efficient Post-Training Quantization for Large Language Models.* ICML 2023, PMLR 202. arXiv:2211.10438. https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10438 — offline per-channel scaling migrates quantization difficulty from outlier activations onto flat weights, making W8A8 INT8 serving accurate to 530B. Dossier: `papers/smoothquant.md`.
- Tseng, A., Chee, J., Sun, Q., Kuleshov, V., & De Sa, C. (2024). *QuIP#: Even Better LLM Quantization with Hadamard Incoherence and Lattice Codebooks.* ICML 2024, PMLR 235. arXiv:2402.04396. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04396 — Randomized-Hadamard incoherence + E8-lattice vector quantization makes 2-bit Llama-2 usable; the conceptual lineage of extreme low-bit practice (not a derivation claim for GGUF's IQ family). Dossier: `papers/quip-sharp.md`.

### Scheduling & disaggregation (wave 4b)

- Agrawal, A., Kedia, N., Panwar, A., Mohan, J., Kwatra, N., Gulavani, B. S., Tumanov, A., & Ramjee, R. (2024). *Taming Throughput-Latency Tradeoff in LLM Inference with Sarathi-Serve.* OSDI '24, 117–134. arXiv:2403.02310. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02310 — chunked prefill + decode-maximal batching under a fixed token budget bounds every step's latency by the budget, not the longest prompt; up to 2.6× serving capacity (Mistral-7B, 1× A100) under P99-TBT SLOs. For a single-user runner: chunking doesn't shorten TTFT — it bounds the stall (Volmarr's 52.1 s prompt silence is this paper's pathology at its own scale). Dossier: `papers/sarathi-serve.md`.
- Zhong, Y., Liu, S., Chen, J., Hu, J., Zhu, Y., Liu, X., Jin, X., & Zhang, H. (2024). *DistServe: Disaggregating Prefill and Decoding for Goodput-optimized Large Language Model Serving.* OSDI '24, 193–210. arXiv:2401.09670. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09670 — phases disaggregated onto separate pools with searched parallelism, bandwidth-aware KV handoff; up to 7.4× more requests or 12.6× tighter SLOs (v3/OSDI figures; arXiv v1 reported 4.48×/10.2× — both recorded in the dossier). Mechanism does not apply at one GPU (its own §7); the TTFT/TPOT budget discipline does. Dossier: `papers/distserve.md`.
- Patel, P., Choukse, E., Zhang, C., Shah, A., Goiri, Í., Maleki, S., & Bianchini, R. (2024). *Splitwise: Efficient Generative LLM Inference Using Phase Splitting.* ISCA 2024, 118–132. DOI: 10.1109/ISCA59077.2024.00019. arXiv:2311.18677. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18677 — characterisation first: prefill is compute-bound, decode memory-bound (H100's 3.43× A100 compute buys only ~0.70× its TBT); phase-split pools reach 1.4× throughput at 20% lower cost on production traces. Dossier: `papers/splitwise.md`.

### Speculative decoding (wave 2)

- Leviathan, Y., Kalman, M., & Matias, Y. (2023). *Fast Inference from Transformers via Speculative Decoding.* ICML 2023, PMLR 202, 19274–19286. arXiv:2211.17192. https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17192 — a small model drafts γ tokens; the target verifies them in one pass under modified rejection sampling that provably preserves the target distribution (2–3×, T5-XXL, TPU-v4). Dossier: `papers/speculative-decoding.md`.
- Chen, C., Borgeaud, S., Irving, G., Lespiau, J.-B., Sifre, L., & Jumper, J. (2023). *Accelerating Large Language Model Decoding with Speculative Sampling.* arXiv:2302.01318. https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.01318 — the independent DeepMind arrival at the same algorithm, scaled to Chinchilla 70B with a purpose-shaped 4B draft (2–2.5×). Dossier: `papers/speculative-decoding.md`.
- Cai, T., Li, Y., Geng, Z., Peng, H., Lee, J. D., Chen, D., & Dao, T. (2024). *Medusa: Simple LLM Inference Acceleration Framework with Multiple Decoding Heads.* ICML 2024. arXiv:2401.10774. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10774 — extra decoding heads on the target's own hidden state + tree attention; no separate draft model (2.2–2.8×, Vicuna-7B). Dossier: `papers/medusa.md`.
- Li, Y., Wei, F., Zhang, C., & Zhang, H. (2024). *EAGLE: Speculative Sampling Requires Rethinking Feature Uncertainty.* ICML 2024. arXiv:2401.15077. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15077 — draft at the feature level with a shifted token input; lossless 2.7–3.5× on LLaMA2-Chat 70B. Dossier: `papers/eagle.md`.
- Li, Y., Wei, F., Zhang, C., & Zhang, H. (2025). *EAGLE-3: Scaling up Inference Acceleration of Large Language Models via Training-Time Test.* NeurIPS 2025. arXiv:2503.01840. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01840 — direct token prediction + multi-layer feature fusion + training-time test; up to 6.5×. Dossier: `papers/eagle-3.md`.
- Fu, Y., Bailis, P., Stoica, I., & Zhang, H. (2024). *Break the Sequential Dependency of LLM Inference Using Lookahead Decoding.* ICML 2024. arXiv:2402.02057. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02057 — Jacobi-iteration n-grams verified in the same pass; no draft model or training (1.8× MT-Bench, A100; heavy extra FLOPs). Dossier: `papers/lookahead-decoding.md`.
- Liu, X., Hu, L., Bailis, P., Cheung, A., Deng, Z., Stoica, I., & Zhang, H. (2024). *Online Speculative Decoding.* ICML 2024, PMLR 235, 31131–31146. arXiv:2310.07177. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07177 — the draft model retrains continually on live traffic from rejection points; acceptance +0.1–0.65, latency ÷1.42–2.17 (final ICML figures). Dossier: `papers/online-speculative-decoding.md`.

## Memory science & allocation

- Wulf, W. A., & McKee, S. A. (1995). *Hitting the Memory Wall: Implications of the Obvious.* ACM SIGARCH Computer Architecture News 23(1). — DRAM latency improving ~7%/yr against CPU ~80%/yr: the wall as the founding observation of memory-centric design. Dossier: `memory/memory-wall-and-roofline.md`.
- McKee, S. A. (2004). *Reflections on the Memory Wall.* — the wall revisited a decade on. Dossier: `memory/memory-wall-and-roofline.md`.
- Williams, S., Waterman, A., & Patterson, D. (2009). *Roofline: An Insightful Visual Performance Model for Multicore Architectures.* Communications of the ACM 52(4). — attainable performance = min(peak compute, bandwidth × arithmetic intensity); the working method for judging whether a kernel is memory-bound. Dossier: `memory/memory-wall-and-roofline.md`.
- McCalpin, J. D. STREAM benchmark + machine-balance literature — sustained bandwidth (not peak) as the honest denominator; latency ≠ bandwidth. Dossier: `memory/memory-wall-and-roofline.md`.
- Yuan, Z., et al. (2024). *LLM Inference Unveiled: Survey and Roofline Model Insights.* arXiv:2402.16363. — GEMM-vs-GEMV arithmetic intensity for LLM inference; batching raises intensity toward the compute roof. Dossier: `memory/memory-wall-and-roofline.md`.
- MimirAlloc campaigns (ours, 2026). Pi campaign final gate 4,590 run / 0 failed; Z13 campaign final gate 4,988 passed / 0 failed / 77 skipped; both 117/117 slices. Records: campaign goal folders + keeper logs, mined in `memory/mimiralloc-our-own-proven-work.md`.
